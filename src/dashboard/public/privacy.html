Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Your privacy is important to The Elite Circle. This Privacy Policy outlines how Elite AI Bot collects, processes, and protects your data when you interact with our Discord bot and dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

When interacting with Elite AI Bot, we may process the following types of information to provide our services:

  • Discord Identifiers: We temporarily process user IDs, guild (server) IDs, and channel IDs to route commands and enforce permissions.
  • Message Content: For AI-related features, the text prompt provided to the bot is processed. We do not proactively read or log messages in channels unless the bot is specifically invoked or mentioned.
  • Dashboard Authentication: For users accessing the dashboard, we use Discord OAuth2 to verify your identity and server management permissions.

2. How We Use the Data

The information collected is used strictly to operate the bot and its dashboard. Specifically, we use it for:

  • Executing moderation commands and configuring server settings.
  • Processing user queries through AI models.
  • Authenticating administrators in the web dashboard.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide advanced functionality, Elite AI Bot integrates with third-party services. The information processed is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Discord API: The core platform we operate on. By using this bot, your data is also handled according to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Groq API: Text prompts intended for the AI are securely transmitted to Groq APIs for generation. We do not send personally identifiable information (PII) beyond the prompt text.

4. Data Retention

Elite AI Bot is designed to minimize data footprint. We do not permanently store message histories or user chat logs. Configuration data (such as server settings or toggle states) is kept only as long as the bot remains in your server. AI prompts are processed in-memory and discarded after a response is generated.

5. Security

We implement standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS for the dashboard and secure bearer token authentication, to protect against unauthorized access or data alteration. However, no internet-based service can guarantee 100% security.

6. User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your server's data. If you wish to revoke the bot's access and clear associated configurations, you can kick the bot from your Discord server. If you need assistance with data removal, please contact The Elite Circle's support team.

7. Policy Changes

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of Elite AI Bot after any changes indicates your acknowledgment and acceptance of the revised terms.
